The Story Behind the Name

The Silence Is Doing Its Own Kind of Work.

Ted and Ixi are professional musicians, concert producers, and artist coaches whose love of nature and community led them to create Pausa.

Pausa is a musical term, the mark in a score that tells the musicians to hold still. The intentional silence is essential to the music. It's what actually makes the next phrase come alive.

We named this collection of properties after that idea, because we think families and friend groups need the same thing music does: a real pause, held on purpose, in a place built for it. Not another item on the itinerary, but a place where the whole point is to stop rushing, sit around the same table, and stay long after the plates are cleared.
